Hello, I'm Prudhvi Bade. I am a technology leader and finance enthusiast. Currently, I serve as a Senior Director (Data and AI Enablement) in Gartner's analyst team.

My professional journey has been centered around data, architecture, and strategy. Previously, I spent over 6 years at The Modern Data Company, moving from Senior Program Manager in the CEO's office to Director roles including Director of Data Engineering and Architecture. Previously, I worked at Google (Program Manager) and Cognizant (Business Strategy Consultant, leading end-to-end deal strategy for Kaiser).

By night, I am a passionate voice in Equity Research. I believe in the power of fundamental analysis and first-principles thinking when it comes to the markets. I write about my findings and market observations on my Substack.

Core Expertise:
- Data Management & AI Enablement: Advising organizations on AI strategy, agentic data management, data observability, and modern data practices.
- Data Architecture & Engineering: Scalable data fabrics, platform engineering, and AI systems.
- Equity Research: Deep dives into company fundamentals and market themes.

Originally a systems engineer at Jio and co-founder of Gharpe.in, I've always been driven by the challenge of building things from scratch and making complex systems work faster and better.

Subheading: A collection of guides for life and engineering.
Items:
- First Principles Thinking: Break down complex problems into their basic elements and reassemble them from the ground up.
- Bias for Action: Speed and iteration are often more valuable than perfect planning. Build, measure, and learn.
- Extreme Ownership: Take full responsibility for results and team outcomes, regardless of the situation.
- Intellectual Honesty: Be willing to admit what you don't know and change your mind when presented with new data.
- Compound Interest: Whether in finance or learning, the greatest results come from consistent effort over a long period.
